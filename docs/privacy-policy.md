## English

Ana Gonda Calculator is an offline-first app for land calculations used in Bangladeshi deeds and khatians — share division, measurement, and unit conversion. All computation happens on the device.

**Data we collect: none.**

- The app does not require an account, sign-in, or device identifier.
- There is no analytics, tracking, advertising, or advertising ID.
- Your calculations, history, and custom rate overrides are stored only in the on-device SQLite database and AsyncStorage; we never transmit them to any server.
- The app requests no permissions.

**Other things to know:**

- The "Copy" action writes result text to your device clipboard only when you tap it.
- Reference links (for example, a government land-survey map check) open pages in your browser; the privacy policy of that site — not this one — applies once you arrive.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored history and settings.
